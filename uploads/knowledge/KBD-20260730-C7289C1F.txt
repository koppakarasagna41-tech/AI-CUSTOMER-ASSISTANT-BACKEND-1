Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the password reset link on the login page. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

Q: Can I change my billing address?
A: Yes. Billing address changes can be submitted from the account settings page.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
