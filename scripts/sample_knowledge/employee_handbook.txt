Employee Handbook

Welcome to our company. All employees must attend onboarding within their first week. Remote work requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior during all company interactions. Personal devices may be used for work only with prior approval from IT.

Attendance and punctuality are required. If an employee is sick, they must notify their manager before the start of the workday. Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR portal.

Employees should protect confidential information and use approved systems for storing company data. The company prohibits harassment, discrimination, and retaliation.
