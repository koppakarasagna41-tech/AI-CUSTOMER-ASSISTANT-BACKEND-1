Product Manual

The Acme Cloud Desk is a productivity device for remote teams. It includes a 27-inch display, wireless keyboard, and built-in webcam. The device connects to Wi-Fi or Ethernet and supports video conferencing applications.

To set up the device, place it on a stable desk, connect the power cable, and turn it on. The first-time setup wizard guides users through Wi-Fi configuration and account sign-in. The device comes with a one-year warranty and includes customer support coverage for hardware defects.

For best performance, keep the device updated with the latest firmware. If the device overheats, disconnect it from power and contact support.
